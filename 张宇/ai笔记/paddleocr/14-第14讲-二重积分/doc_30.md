## 3 极坐标方程型

考生应能熟练画出平面区域 D.

(1)

 $$ D=\left\{(r,\theta)\left|0\leqslant r\leqslant\frac{1}{\sin\theta},\frac{\pi}{4}\leqslant\theta\leqslant\frac{\pi}{2}\right.\right\} $$ 

(2)

 $$ D=\left\{(r,\theta)\middle|0\leqslant r\leqslant\sec\theta,0\leqslant\theta\leqslant\frac{\pi}{4}\right\} $$

(3)

 $$ D=\left\{(r,\theta)\middle|0\leq\theta\leq2\pi,\frac{\theta}{2}\leq r\leq\pi\right\}. $$ 

(4)

 $$ D=\left\{(r,\theta)\middle|0\leqslant r\leqslant1,0\leqslant\theta\leqslant\frac{\pi}{4}\right\}. $$

 $$ D=\left\{(r,\theta)\middle|\frac{1}{\sqrt{\sin\theta\cos\theta}}\leq r\leq\frac{2}{\cos\theta}\right., $$ 

(6)

 $$ D=\left\{(r,\theta)\middle|2\leqslant r\leqslant2(1+\cos\theta),-\frac{\pi}{2}\leqslant\theta\leqslant\frac{\pi}{2}\right\} $$ 

 $$ \arctan\frac{1}{4}\leqslant\theta\leqslant\frac{\pi}{4}\} $$

 $$ D=\left\{(r,\theta)\left|0\leqslant r\leqslant\sqrt{\sin2\theta},0\leqslant\theta\leqslant\frac{\pi}{2}\right.\right\}. $$

 $$ D=\left\{(r,\theta)\middle|0\leqslant\theta\leqslant\frac{\pi}{2},0\leqslant r\leqslant2\cos\theta\right., $$
