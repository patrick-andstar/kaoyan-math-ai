 $$ \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}=\frac{y_{t}^{\prime}}{x_{t}^{\prime}}=\frac{3\sin^{2}t\cos t}{-3\cos^{2}t\sin t}=-\left.\tan t,\right.\left.\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}\right|_{t=\frac{\pi}{4}}=-1, $$ 

 $$ \frac{\mathrm{d}^{2}y}{\mathrm{d}x^{2}}=(-\tan t)^{\prime}\frac{\mathrm{d}t}{\mathrm{d}x}=-\frac{1}{\cos^{2}t}\cdot\frac{1}{-3\cos^{2}t\sin t}=\frac{1}{3\cos^{4}t\sin t}\cdot\left.\frac{\mathrm{d}^{2}y}{\mathrm{d}x^{2}}\right|_{t=\frac{\pi}{4}}=\frac{4}{3}\sqrt{2}\cdot $$ 

按曲率公式， $ t=\frac{\pi}{4} $ 对应点处的曲率为

 $$ k=\frac{\left|\frac{\mathrm{d}^{2}y}{\mathrm{d}x^{2}}\right|}{\left[1+\left(\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}\right)^{2}\right]^{3/2}}\Bigg|_{t=\frac{\pi}{4}}=\frac{\frac{4}{3}\sqrt{2}}{2^{3/2}}=\frac{2}{3}. $$
