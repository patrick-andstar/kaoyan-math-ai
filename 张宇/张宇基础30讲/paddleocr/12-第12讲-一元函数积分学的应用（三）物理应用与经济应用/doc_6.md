如图 12-10 所示，当质量系统为连续可求面积的平面有界闭区域时，有

 $$ \Delta M_{i}=\rho(\xi_{i},\eta_{i})\Delta\sigma_{i},\Delta M_{i L_{0}}=r_{i}\rho(\xi_{i},\eta_{i})\Delta\sigma_{i}, $$ 

记  $ \lambda = \max_{i} \{\Delta \sigma_{i}\} $，则总力矩为

 $$ \begin{aligned}M_{L_{0}}=&\lim_{\lambda\rightarrow0}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\Delta M_{i l_{0}}=\lim_{\lambda\rightarrow0}\sum_{i=1}^{n}r_{i}\rho(\xi_{i},\eta_{i})\Delta\sigma_{i}=\iint\limits_{D}r(x,y)\rho(x,y)\mathrm{d}\sigma\\=&\iint\limits_{D}\frac{\left|a x+b y+c\right|}{\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}}}\rho(x,y)\mathrm{d}\sigma.\end{aligned} $$ 

当 $ \rho= $常数时，则有

 $$ r(\overline{x},\overline{y})=\frac{M_{L_{0}}}{M}=\frac{\iint\limits_{D}\frac{\left|ax+by+c\right|}{\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}}}\mathrm{d}\sigma}{\iint\limits_{D}\mathrm{d}\sigma}, $$ 

其中  $ r(\bar{x}, \bar{y}) $ 为形心  $ (\bar{x}, \bar{y}) $ 到直线 L 的距离.
